# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ProphyLens welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, sexualised attention, insults, trolling, publishing private information, or conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainer through the security/contact route listed in the GitHub profile or repository security policy. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.
